## MusicXML schemas

`skills/score-transcription/assets/musicxml-schema/` contains schemas from the
W3C MusicXML 4.0 release. Original copyright and licensing notices are retained
inside each file. Source: https://github.com/w3c/musicxml/tree/v4.0/schema .

## Score examples

The Luonnotar source crops come from the user-supplied IMSLP piano reduction of
Jean Sibelius's *Luonnotar*, Op. 70. Finnish lyrics derive from the *Kalevala*.
The corroborating orchestral crop is Breitkopf PB 5076, IMSLP #1025940,
PDF page 30. Associated MusicXML and JSON are Kengraver transcriptions of the
identified excerpts, with excerpt boundaries recorded in their data.

The owner authorized sharing these as public-domain scores. This package does
not independently certify copyright status in every jurisdiction or claim MIT
rights over the underlying compositions, editions or translations. Full scans
are not bundled. Source locations and the scope of review appear in the lessons
and example data. The full piano scan's IMSLP item number was not retained in
the pilot metadata; attribution should be refined if that edition is identified.

## External tools

MuseScore, Audiveris, Tesseract language data, lxml and PyMuPDF are separately
distributed upstream dependencies under their own licenses. Their binaries are
not bundled. The bootstrap manifest records upstream URLs and pinned hashes.
